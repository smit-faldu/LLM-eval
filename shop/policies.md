# Returns and Refunds
Delivered orders can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days we cannot offer a refund, but items may still be covered by warranty. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear. Each order can be refunded only once.

# Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled only while their status is pending. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; wait for delivery and request a return instead. Cancelled orders are refunded in full automatically within 3 business days.

# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 pay a flat $4.99 shipping fee. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $12.99. We currently ship only within the country; international shipping is not available.

# Warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date covering defects, not accidental damage or water damage. To claim warranty, contact support with your order ID and a description or photo of the defect. Warranty claims are repaired or replaced, not refunded.

# Payment
We accept credit cards, debit cards, PayPal and UPI. Payment is charged when the order is placed. We do not store full card numbers. Cash on delivery is not available.

# Out of Stock Items
Out-of-stock products cannot be ordered. Restock dates are not guaranteed; customers can check the product page again later. We do not take backorders or pre-orders.
