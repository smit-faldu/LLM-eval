# Returns and Refunds
Delivered orders can be returned for a full refund of the item cost within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days we cannot offer a refund, but items may still be covered by warranty. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we shipped the wrong item. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear. Each order can be refunded only once, and a refund always covers the whole order.

# Non-Returnable Items
Screen protectors, opened microSD cards and USB flash drives, and items damaged by the customer cannot be returned. Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.

# Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. To swap an item for a different model or colour, request a refund within the 30-day window and place a new order.

# Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled only while their status is pending. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; wait for delivery and request a return instead. Cancelled orders are refunded in full, including shipping, automatically within 3 business days.

# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders of $50 or more. Standard orders under $50 pay a flat $4.99 fee. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $12.99. We currently ship only within India; international shipping is not available. Every shipped order gets a tracking number.

# Late or Lost Deliveries
If a standard order has not arrived 7 business days after shipping, or an express order after 3 business days, contact support with your order ID and we will open a shipping ticket with the courier. Lost packages are reshipped or refunded in full, including shipping.

# Damaged on Arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will open a damaged-item ticket and send a free replacement or a full refund including shipping. Damage reported after 48 hours is handled as a warranty claim.

# Warranty
Every product has a manufacturer warranty, measured from the delivery date. The length depends on the product and ranges from 6 to 36 months; screen protectors have no warranty. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage, water damage or normal wear. To claim warranty, contact support with your order ID and a description or photo of the defect; we open a warranty ticket. Warranty claims are repaired or replaced, never refunded.

# Membership Tiers
Customers have one of three tiers: regular, silver or gold. Silver members get free standard shipping on every order with no minimum. Gold members get free standard and free express shipping, plus priority support. Tier is reviewed every year based on spending: silver above $500 per year, gold above $1500 per year.

# Price Match
If you find the same product cheaper at a major Indian retailer within 7 days of buying, we refund the difference once per order. Marketplace sellers, flash sales and refurbished listings do not qualify.

# Payment
We accept credit cards, debit cards, PayPal, UPI and gift cards. Payment is charged when the order is placed. We do not store full card numbers. Cash on delivery is not available. EMI on credit cards is available for orders above $200.

# Out of Stock Items
Out-of-stock products cannot be ordered. Restock dates are not guaranteed; customers can check the product page again later. We do not take backorders or pre-orders.

# Support Tickets
Support tickets track warranty claims, damaged items, shipping problems, billing issues and other requests. Tickets move from open to in progress to resolved. Typical first response time is 24 hours, or 4 hours for gold members.

# Privacy
We use customer data only to process orders and provide support. We never sell personal data. Customers can ask support to delete their account and data at any time; order records required for tax are kept for 8 years.
